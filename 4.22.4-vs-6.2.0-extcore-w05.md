# FalkorDB 4.22.4 vs 6.2.0 — medium extended-core + 5% writes

Date: 2026-11-22  
Dataset: medium Pokec (100,000 nodes / 1,768,515 `Friend` edges)  
Workload: `medium-extended-core-w05-falkor` — 10,000 queries, **extended-core**, **~5.1% writes** (513)  
Config: `parallel=8`, `mps=2000`, `FALKOR_QUERY_TIMEOUT_MS=20000`, algos off  
Includes lightened deep hops (`exact_5/6` with `LIMIT 100`) and other catalog caps  
Results: `Results-261122-4.22.4-vs-6.2.0-extcore-w05/`

### Verdict

**6.2.0 remains ahead on extended-core + 5% writes:** −18% wall time, +21% QPS, −17% avg, **much better p99** (−44%), **zero errors** on both. The gap is **smaller than baseline+w05** (baseline was ~−30% elapsed / +43% QPS): extended-core’s heavier read/path mix dilutes pure write-path wins.

Deep hops with `LIMIT 100` stay at **0% fail** on both (no unbounded 5/6-hop timeout class).

## Bottom line

Same medium setup as baseline+w05, but **extended-core**: **6.2.0 still wins** on elapsed and throughput with perfect reliability; absolute improvement vs 4.22.4 is solid but **not as large** as on pure baseline+writes.
