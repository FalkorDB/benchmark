### Verdict (headline)

**6.2.0 is substantially faster end-to-end on medium baseline + 5% writes:** ~**30% less wall time**, ~**43% higher QPS**, **much better p99** (−63%), with **zero errors** on both sides.

Tradeoffs:

- **p50 slightly higher** on 6.2.0 (~+13%)
- **p95 higher** (~+116%) — mid-tail shape differs; still dominated by path-ish queries
- **Slightly more reported graph memory** (125 vs 103 MB)

---

## What improved most on 6.2.0

Write path is a clear win (matches prior lock/poll findings on older lines):

| Query (writes) | notes |
|----------------|-------|
| `single_edge_update` and other mutations | large **p99 drops** on 6.2.0 (often ~50–60%+) |

Also strong **tail** improvement on global p99 (561 → 210 ms), consistent with fewer multi-hundred-ms stalls under mixed R/W load.

## Where 6.2.0 looks slower (query p99)

Some **read** shapes show higher p99 on 6.2.0 even while overall throughput wins, for example:

- `shortest_path_with_filter` / related path helpers — higher p99
- `entity_path_introspection` — higher p99
- selected aggregation / expansion queries — mixed p50/p99

None of these produced failures under the 20s timeout.

## Bottom line

On medium **baseline + ~5% writes**, **FalkorDB 6.2.0 outperforms 4.22.4** on wall-clock, throughput, average latency, and p99, with identical reliability (0 errors). Expect a small p50/p95 and memory uptick; write-heavy tails are much healthier on 6.2.0.
